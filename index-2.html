About me

A little about how I got here

I grew up taking apart old radios to see how they worked, which turned into a habit of taking apart ideas instead. Today that means studying Computer Science, building small tools that solve real problems, and spending too much time perfecting the details no one else notices.

Outside of coursework, I volunteer teaching basic coding to high school students, and I'm part of my university's robotics club, where I mostly handle the software side of things.

I care about writing code that's easy for the next person to read — including future me.
